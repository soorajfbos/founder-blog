## What Founders Should Look for in a Technical Due Diligence Review

A startup's technology can have a major effect on its future development, operating costs, security, and ability to scale. However, founders may not always have enough technical information to understand the condition of their product.

A structured technical due diligence review can provide a clearer picture of the technology and highlight areas that may need attention.

### Review the Codebase

The review should examine the structure, quality, complexity, and maintainability of the code.

This can help identify areas where future development may require more time or resources than expected.

### Assess the Architecture

Software architecture affects how easily a product can handle new features and increasing usage.

Founders should understand whether the current architecture supports the product's business goals and future requirements.

### Check Technical Debt

Technical debt can develop through shortcuts, outdated technology, or decisions made under time pressure.

A [technical due diligence startup](https://foundersbar.com/articles-and-research/technical-debt-in-startups) review can help identify technical debt and explain how it may affect future development and maintenance.

### Examine Security

Security should be reviewed across the application, infrastructure, databases, authentication systems, and third-party services.

The goal is to identify weaknesses that could create risks for the business or its users.

### Review Infrastructure

Cloud infrastructure, databases, storage, monitoring, and deployment systems should be examined to determine whether they are suitable for the product's current needs.

### Evaluate Development Practices

The review can examine testing, version control, deployment, documentation, code review, and development workflows.

Strong processes can make future development more predictable and easier to manage.

### Check Third-Party Dependencies

External APIs, software libraries, cloud services, and other vendors can become important parts of a product.

Founders should understand which dependencies are critical and whether they could create technical or operational risks.

### Assess Scalability

The product should be reviewed against expected growth.

Potential limitations involving databases, infrastructure, application performance, or architecture should be identified before they become major problems.

### Understand Future Costs

Technical issues can create future expenses through additional development, infrastructure upgrades, maintenance, security improvements, or hiring.

A good review should help founders understand these potential requirements.

### Turn Findings Into Actions

Technical due diligence is most useful when the findings lead to clear next steps.

Founders can prioritize issues based on business impact, technical risk, development effort, and urgency. This creates a practical plan for improving the product while supporting future growth.
