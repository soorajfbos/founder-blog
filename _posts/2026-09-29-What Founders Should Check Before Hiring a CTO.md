# What Founders Should Check Before Hiring a CTO

Hiring a CTO is an important step for a startup that needs stronger technical leadership. The right timing and role definition can help the company manage technology while continuing to grow.

Before hiring, founders should understand what the business actually needs from its technical leader.

## Define the CTO's Responsibilities

The CTO role can vary between startups.

Some companies need someone focused on architecture and engineering, while others need leadership across product technology, security, infrastructure, hiring, and technical strategy.

Defining these responsibilities first can make the hiring process more focused.

## Review the Current Technology

Founders should understand the current condition of their technology before hiring.

This can include the software architecture, codebase, infrastructure, security practices, technical debt, development process, and major third-party dependencies.

## Identify the Main Technical Problems

A CTO should have clear problems to solve.

Founders can list the biggest technical challenges facing the company, such as slow development, reliability issues, scaling concerns, poor documentation, or difficulty managing an external development team.

This helps candidates understand the actual situation.

## Consider the Company's Growth Stage

The technical leadership needed by an early-stage startup may differ from what a larger company requires.

Founders should consider the current team size, product maturity, customer base, funding plans, and expected growth when defining the role.

## Check Leadership Experience

Technical skills are only one part of the CTO role.

Depending on the startup's needs, the founder may also require someone who can manage engineers, work with vendors, communicate with investors, support product decisions, and establish technical processes.

## Review Communication Skills

A CTO often needs to explain technical decisions to people without an engineering background.

Clear communication can help founders understand risks, costs, trade-offs, and technical priorities before making important business decisions.

## Understand What Support Is Needed

Not every startup needs a full-time CTO immediately.

Some founders may need technical leadership for a specific period, such as preparing for growth, reviewing an existing product, managing vendors, or planning an engineering team.

In situations like these, [fractional cto services for startups](https://foundersbar.com/articles-and-research/when-to-hire-a-fractional-cto-vs-full-time-cto) can provide technical leadership without immediately creating a full-time executive role.

## Create Clear Expectations

Before hiring a CTO, founders should define what success will look like.

Clear goals around product development, technical reliability, team building, security, infrastructure, or technical planning can help both sides understand the role.

A CTO should solve the technical problems that matter most to the business. By defining the role, reviewing current technology, and setting clear expectations, founders can create a stronger foundation for technical leadership.
