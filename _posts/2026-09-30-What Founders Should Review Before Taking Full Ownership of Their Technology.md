# What Founders Should Review Before Taking Full Ownership of Their Technology

Some startups begin with an external development agency, freelancers, or a small group of contractors. As the company grows, founders may decide to bring more technology work under their direct control.

Taking ownership of the technology can improve visibility and decision-making, but the transition needs careful planning.

## Understand the Current Technology

Before changing the development setup, founders should understand what has already been built.

This includes the application architecture, database, hosting environment, integrations, APIs, development tools, and third-party services.

A clear technical overview helps identify important dependencies before responsibilities are transferred.

## Review Code and Documentation

The development team should have access to the source code and relevant technical documentation.

Founders should also confirm that documentation explains important setup, deployment, database, and maintenance processes.

Missing documentation can make the transition harder for a new team.

## Check Access and Ownership

Technology ownership is not only about having the source code.

Founders should review access to repositories, cloud platforms, domains, databases, analytics tools, payment systems, APIs, and other important accounts.

The company should know who controls these accounts and how access can be transferred when necessary.

## Review Technical Debt

Older systems may contain shortcuts or temporary solutions that were created during early development.

Before taking ownership, founders should understand which technical issues could create future maintenance problems.

Not every issue needs to be fixed immediately, but important risks should be documented and prioritized.

## Understand Current Development Costs

Founders should review how much the existing technology costs to operate and maintain.

This may include development expenses, cloud infrastructure, software subscriptions, third-party services, and support costs.

Understanding these expenses can help with future technology budgeting.

## Check Security Practices

Security should also be reviewed during the transition.

Founders should understand how user data is protected, how access is managed, how backups are handled, and whether important dependencies are regularly updated.

Any known security concerns should be documented before responsibilities change.

## Create a Transition Plan

Moving technology ownership should be treated as a structured process rather than a single handover.

Support such as [interim cto services](https://foundersbar.com/articles-and-research/when-to-hire-a-fractional-cto-vs-full-time-cto) can help founders review the existing setup, organize technical information, and create a practical transition plan.

## Plan What Happens Next

After taking ownership, founders should have a clear plan for development and maintenance.

This may include hiring internal developers, continuing with selected vendors, improving documentation, addressing technical debt, and setting future technology priorities.

A well-planned transition can give founders better visibility into their technology while helping the development team work from a clearer technical foundation.
