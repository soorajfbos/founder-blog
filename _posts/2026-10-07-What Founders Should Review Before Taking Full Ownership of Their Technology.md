# What Founders Should Review Before Taking Full Ownership of Their Technology

Taking full ownership of a startup's technology is an important step. This may happen when a company moves away from an external development agency, changes development partners, or builds an internal technical team.

Before making the transition, founders should make sure they have control over the important parts of the product.

## Confirm Code Ownership

Founders should confirm that the company has ownership and access to the source code.

The code should be stored in an account controlled by the company rather than depending on a developer's personal account.

## Check Cloud Access

Cloud platforms often contain important parts of the product.

Founders should have appropriate administrative access to hosting, databases, storage, monitoring, and other infrastructure services.

## Review Domain and Service Accounts

Important accounts can include domains, email services, payment platforms, analytics tools, authentication systems, and third-party APIs.

The company should maintain control of these accounts and know who currently has access.

## Understand the Technology Stack

Founders should know which technologies are being used to operate the product.

This includes programming languages, frameworks, databases, hosting platforms, external services, and important dependencies.

## Review the Architecture

Before taking full ownership, founders should understand how the main parts of the product work together.

A technical architecture review can identify areas that may require attention after the transition.

## Check Documentation

The development team should provide useful documentation covering deployment, infrastructure, integrations, databases, technical decisions, and known issues.

Good documentation makes it easier for a new team to take responsibility.

## Identify Technical Debt

Founders should understand which technical problems already exist.

Unresolved bugs, outdated dependencies, complicated code, and temporary workarounds can create future development costs.

For startups that need independent technical guidance during this transition, [cto as a service for startups](https://foundersbar.com/articles-and-research/when-to-hire-a-fractional-cto-vs-full-time-cto) can help review the technology, identify risks, and create a practical transition plan.

## Review Security

Access permissions should be reviewed when ownership changes.

Former developers or vendors should not retain unnecessary access to company systems, customer data, or infrastructure.

## Plan Future Technical Support

Taking ownership does not mean the founder needs to manage every technical task personally.

The startup should decide who will handle development, maintenance, infrastructure, security, and future technical decisions.

Taking control of technology is easier when founders know what they own, how the product works, and which risks still need attention. A structured technical review can help create a smoother transition and give the company greater control over its future development.
