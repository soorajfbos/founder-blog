## A Weekly Founder Review That Keeps Your MVP On Track

A short, steady review each week keeps your startup honest. It shows what is working, what is not, and where to focus next. You do not need long decks. You need one dashboard, a simple agenda, and clear decisions.

### Set A 30-Minute Agenda

Keep the same flow each week:
- 5 minutes: metrics review
- 10 minutes: user learnings
- 10 minutes: delivery and risks
- 5 minutes: decisions and owners

Start on time. End on time. Take notes in one doc. Share with the team right after.

### Use One Dashboard

Track five numbers that tell the story:
- Sign ups per week from target users
- Activation by day 7
- Weekly active users
- Time to first value
- Retention or paid conversion

Add uptime and error rate if your app is live. Keep definitions on the page so no one argues about what each number means.

### Bring Real User Signals

Share short clips or quotes from the last 5 calls. Show where users struggled and where they smiled. List the top two requests. Decide which one change would help most this week. Talking to users beats guessing.

### Check Delivery With Facts

Review the last cycle:
- What shipped
- What slipped and why
- The top two blockers

If a task is stuck, cut scope or provide help. Do not carry dead tasks forward. Protect focus for the small set of bets that move your main metric.

### Make And Log Decisions

End with a clear list:
- What we will stop
- What we will start
- Who owns each item and by when

Keep a decision log with date, choice, and reason. Link to data or user notes. This saves time in future debates and helps new hires get context fast.

### Share A Simple Investor Update

Send a two-paragraph note each month:
- What we learned and what we shipped
- Where numbers moved and what we will try next

Attach your dashboard. Ask for one clear help. Speed and honesty build trust with backers.

### Plan Experiments, Not Big Swings

Each week pick one small test:
- A faster onboarding step
- A clearer email
- A new hint in the main flow

Time-box to a few days. Measure the result. Keep wins. Kill losses. This steady drumbeat finds product market fit faster than big rewrites.

### What To Do When Numbers Dip

- Find the moment users drop in the flow
- Watch 5 sessions or calls
- Ship one fix in a week
- Recheck the dashboard next week

Do not add more features when basics are broken. Fix the path to value first.
