## Your Final Checklist Before You Scale

### Before you grow, confirm:
- [ ] Your core metric is healthy
- [ ] Your code is stable and tested
- [ ] You have backups and security basics
- [ ] Your team knows the product vision
- [ ] You have a budget plan for growth
- [ ] You have a technical leader or advisor
- [ ] You have a simple documentation process

### Start with one change
Do not try to fix everything at once. Pick the most important improvement and make it real.

### Monitor and adjust
Growth brings new problems. Watch your metrics and your team energy. Adjust quickly.

### Remember
You do not need to be perfect. You need to be ready. Use [fractional cto services for startups](https://foundersbar.com/articles-and-research/top-strategies-for-effective-startup-software-development) when you need expert help without a full-time cost.

### Final thought
Scaling is a series of smart steps. Plan well, hire slowly, and stay focused. Your product will grow with you.
